EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS) - the effect of an explicit ESCAPE '\' clause on the village search
Date: 2026-11-23   Data: 20014 villages (20000 perf- seed=1), 26068 village_names, 4 trigram GIN indexes, ANALYZE run
Code: perf/k6-village-search with the escaped pattern; the queries are the UNION count query of the search.

Two runs of the same script, the first with ESCAPE '\' on every ILIKE, the second without the clause (the default
escape character of PostgreSQL is already the backslash). Section 1 uses literal patterns; section 2 uses a prepared
statement with a bound parameter and plan_cache_mode = force_generic_plan (how the application sends the query, and the
case where the planner knows least). Section 3 shows that the results are the same.
